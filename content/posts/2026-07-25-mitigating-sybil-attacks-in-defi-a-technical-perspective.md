# Mitigating Sybil Attacks in DeFi: A Technical Perspective
## Introduction
Sybil attacks pose a significant threat to decentralized finance (DeFi) applications, compromising the integrity of on-chain voting and decision-making processes.

## The Engineering Challenge
Traditional solutions rely on manual verification, which is time-consuming and prone to errors. Moreover, relying solely on reputation systems can be vulnerable to manipulation.

## The Current State of Sybil Resistance
Most DeFi applications employ a combination of IP blocking, rate limiting, and manual verification to mitigate Sybil attacks. However, these measures are often insufficient, and the burden of manual verification can be overwhelming.

## A Technical Solution
Trestle's decentralized identity solution provides a robust and scalable approach to sybil resistance. By leveraging zero-knowledge proofs and cryptographic techniques, Trestle enables secure and efficient verification of identities, eliminating the need for manual intervention.
