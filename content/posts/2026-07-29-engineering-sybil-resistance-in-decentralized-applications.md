# Introduction

Decentralized applications (dApps) have become increasingly popular in the DeFi space. However, they are often plagued by a common issue: Sybil resistance. This refers to the ability of a single entity to create multiple fake identities or accounts, which can be used to manipulate the system or game the rules.

# The Problem

Sybil resistance is a significant challenge for dApps. It can lead to a variety of problems, including:

* Manipulation of voting systems
* Gaming of rewards or incentives
* Creation of fake accounts to spam or harass others

# The Current State of Affairs

Traditional solutions to Sybil resistance rely on manual verification processes, such as requiring users to upload identification documents or complete CAPTCHAs. However, these approaches are often cumbersome and can drive away users.
