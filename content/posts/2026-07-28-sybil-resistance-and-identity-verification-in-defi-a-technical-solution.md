# Sybil Resistance and Identity Verification in DeFi: A Technical Solution

In decentralized finance (DeFi), ensuring the integrity of user identities and preventing Sybil attacks is crucial for maintaining the trust and security of the ecosystem.

## The Problem

Sybil attacks occur when a malicious actor creates multiple fake identities to manipulate the system, often for personal gain. This can lead to a loss of trust in the platform and undermine its overall security.

## The Engineering Approach

One approach to mitigating Sybil attacks is to implement a robust identity verification system. This can be achieved through a combination of automated and manual verification processes.

For example, a decentralized identity platform can utilize in-channel puzzle verification to confirm a user's identity. When a user joins a channel, they are presented with a puzzle that must be solved to verify their identity. This approach not only prevents Sybil attacks but also ensures that users are actively engaged with the platform.
