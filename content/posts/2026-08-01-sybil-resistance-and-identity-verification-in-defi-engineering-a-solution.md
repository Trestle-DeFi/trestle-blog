  ## Introduction

  Decentralized finance (DeFi) applications are increasingly vulnerable to sybil attacks, where malicious actors create multiple fake identities to manipulate the system. In this post, we'll explore the engineering approach to sybil resistance and identity verification in DeFi.

  ## The Problem: Sybil Attacks in DeFi

  Sybil attacks are a significant concern in DeFi, as they can compromise the integrity of the system. For instance, a malicious actor can create multiple fake identities to manipulate the voting process or gain unfair advantages in liquidity provision.

  ## Standard Tools and Their Limitations

  Traditional solutions to sybil resistance rely on manual verification processes, which are time-consuming and prone to errors. Additionally, many DeFi applications rely on centralized identity providers, which can compromise user anonymity and data security.

  ## A Technical Solution: Trestle's Approach to Sybil Resistance

  Our solution leverages a decentralized identity management system, which enables users to control their identity data and prevents sybil attacks. We've developed a novel approach to identity verification, which combines on-chain data with off-chain social proof.
