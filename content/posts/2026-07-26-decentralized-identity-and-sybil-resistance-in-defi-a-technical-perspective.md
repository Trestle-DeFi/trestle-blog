# Introduction
In DeFi, the threat of Sybil attacks and the need for robust user verification are pressing concerns. These attacks compromise the integrity of decentralized applications, undermining trust and security.

# The Problem of Sybil Attacks
Sybil attacks occur when an attacker creates multiple fake identities or accounts to manipulate the system. This can lead to vote manipulation, spam, and other malicious activities. Traditional solutions often rely on centralized identity verification, which defeats the purpose of decentralized systems.

# Engineering Approach to Sybil Resistance
To address this challenge, we need a decentralized identity solution that ensures the authenticity of user identities. This involves implementing a robust verification process that prevents Sybil attacks. One approach is to use a combination of cryptographic techniques, such as zero-knowledge proofs and digital signatures, to verify user identities without revealing sensitive information.
